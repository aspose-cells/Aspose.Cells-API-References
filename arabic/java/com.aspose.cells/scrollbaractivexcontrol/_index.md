**Example**

```

         //Add a ToggleButtonActiveXControl.

         ScrollBarActiveXControl activeXControl = (ScrollBarActiveXControl)shape.getActiveXControl();
 
         //do your business
 
         //Save the excel file.
         book.save("exmaple.xlsx");
```
